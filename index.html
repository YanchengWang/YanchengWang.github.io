Biography

Yancheng Wang recently completed his Ph.D. in Computer Science at Arizona State University, advised by Prof. Yingzhen Yang. His research focuses on LLM reasoning and agents, LLM post-training, multimodal learning, efficient and robust deep learning, and generative models. He has research experience at Meta Superintelligence Labs, Amazon AWS AI, Amazon Alexa AI, and Kuaishou U.S. R&D Center. His work has been published in leading venues including ICML, ICLR, NeurIPS, ACL, TPAMI, IJCV, UAI, NAACL, and AAAI.
